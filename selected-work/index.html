Government Strategy & Transformation

Two featured engagements

Public Sector · GCC

Centre of Government & Delivery Unit Design

Context

A government with clear national priorities but no reliable way of knowing, at the centre, whether they were actually being delivered — and no mechanism for intervening when they were not.

My role

Designed how the centre of government would function: the mandate of the executive office, what the delivery unit owned versus what line ministries owned, and how performance conversations would run.

Scope

  • Centre-of-government mandate and operating model
  • Delivery unit and PMO / SMO design
  • Performance-management and escalation mechanisms
  • Governance forums, reporting cadence and decision rights

Impact

Gave the centre a single, current view of priority delivery and a standing route for escalating programmes that had gone off track.

Public Sector · GCC

Whole-of-Government Operating Model

Context

Priorities that cut across several ministries kept stalling in the gaps between them — everyone was accountable for a piece, nobody for the whole.

My role

Worked out how ministries and central entities should coordinate, where decisions should sit, and how shared priorities would be owned end to end rather than by fragment.

Scope

  • Cross-government governance and decision rights
  • Institutional mandates and interfaces
  • Shared-priority ownership and coordination mechanisms
  • Implementation roadmap and transition

Impact

Defined a cross-government model to replace ad-hoc coordination with clear ownership and decision rights for shared priorities.

Economic & Public Policy

Two featured engagements

Taxation · Public Policy · Middle East

Corporate & Personal Tax Policy Reform

Context

A government introducing significant changes to its tax regime, where the policy design and the institutional capacity to administer it had to be built in parallel.

My role

Worked on the shape of the reform itself and on the frameworks required to make it operable — what had to exist institutionally before the policy could take effect.

Scope

  • Corporate and personal tax policy design
  • Implementation frameworks and sequencing
  • Institutional readiness and administration model
  • Stakeholder engagement across government

Impact

Moved the reform from policy intent to an implementable programme with a clear owner, sequence and set of institutional prerequisites.

Research & Innovation · Public Sector · GCC

National R&D and Innovation Governance

Context

National ambitions in research and innovation spread across a crowded landscape of institutions with overlapping mandates and no clear centre of gravity.

My role

Set out who does what across the ecosystem — mandates, governance, funding flows and the coordination mechanisms that hold them together.

Scope

  • Innovation governance framework
  • Institutional mandates and operating models
  • Stakeholder-coordination mechanisms
  • Prioritisation and funding governance

Impact

Established a governance model designed to reduce overlap between institutions and give the ecosystem a single structure for setting and tracking national R&D priorities.

Financial Services Transformation

One featured engagement — second in progress

Banking · Financial Services · Middle East

Banking-Sector Reform Programme

Context

A banking sector under pressure, where restoring confidence depended on institutional reform and compliance holding together rather than being fixed one at a time.

My role

Supported the reform programme across governance, compliance and institutional transformation, and on the implementation mechanics that carried it.

Scope

  • Governance and institutional reform
  • Compliance framework and remediation
  • Operating model and organisational design
  • Implementation planning and tracking

Impact

Brought governance, compliance and institutional change under one sequenced programme rather than a set of parallel workstreams.

Corporate & TMT Strategy

One featured engagement

Telecom · M&A · Middle East

Acquisition Strategy & Investment Case

Context

A major telecom operator pursuing growth through acquisition and needing a defensible strategic rationale, not just a valuation.

My role

Built the strategic framework and investment case underpinning the transactions, and the argument that had to survive board and investor scrutiny.

Scope

  • Investment case and value creation logic
  • Market and competitive assessment
  • Board-level decision materials

Impact

Underpinned more than US$5 billion in acquisitions.

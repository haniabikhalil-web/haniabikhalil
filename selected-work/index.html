Public Sector

Government Transformation

A government entity needed a clearer way to coordinate delivery across departments.

Result: a governance structure and operating model the organization still runs on.

›

Context

A government entity's departments operated with overlapping mandates and no shared decision-making structure, slowing delivery of cross-cutting priorities.

My Role

Workstream lead responsible for governance design and stakeholder alignment.

Scope

Designed the governance structure, operating model, and decision rights across departments, and defined the transformation program to implement them.

Impact

The organization adopted the new governance structure and operating model, giving departments a shared mechanism to coordinate and escalate decisions.

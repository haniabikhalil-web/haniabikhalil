About Hani Abi Khalil

Two sides of the same person: the work, and everything that happens around it.

Economist by training

I'm an economist by training who ended up in strategy consulting almost by accident, and stayed because the work kept getting more interesting.

I started at Strategy& in Beirut, working across the GCC on some of my first government and public-sector mandates. From there I moved to Booz Allen Hamilton, then to Oliver Wyman in Dubai — each move taking me deeper into the kind of work I actually wanted to do: helping governments and large institutions figure out how strategy, governance and delivery should actually work together, rather than just producing a strategy document and moving on.

Along the way, my focus sharpened around a few themes that keep showing up regardless of the client: government strategy and transformation, economic and public policy, and financial-services reform — usually some combination of the three, since in the Middle East they rarely come apart cleanly.

In 2024 I took time out to do my MBA at INSEAD, as a sponsored LIFE scholarship recipient — partly to step back and think more broadly about strategy beyond the Middle East, and partly because I wanted the space to think about where I wanted to take my career next (which, alongside consulting, is also where the coaching side of what I do took shape).

I'm known for stakeholder management, collaborative leadership, and for pushing engagements toward implementation rather than stopping at the recommendation.

Career & Education

How I got here

After a B.Sc. in Economics at Saint Joseph University in Lebanon (2016–2019), an Erasmus+ exchange took me to the University of Turin in 2019 — my first real stretch living outside Lebanon, and the point where working abroad stopped being a hypothetical.

I joined Strategy& that same year as an Associate, based in Beirut and working across the GCC. In 2021 I moved to Booz Allen Hamilton as a Consultant, still across Beirut and the GCC, before joining Oliver Wyman in Dubai in 2022 as a Senior Consultant — alongside a Certificate of Professional Advancement from LSE that same year.

2024 was my MBA year at INSEAD, split between Singapore and France, as a sponsored LIFE scholarship recipient — a genuinely global cohort, and a useful reset before the next stretch of my career.

I returned to Oliver Wyman in Dubai in 2025 as an Associate Manager, where I stayed through 2026.

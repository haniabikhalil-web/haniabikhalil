About Hani Abi Khalil

Two sides of the same person: the work, and everything that happens around it.

Economist by training

I'm an economist turned strategy consultant with experience across the Middle East, having worked with Booz Allen Hamilton, Strategy& and Oliver Wyman. My work has focused primarily on government strategy and transformation, economic and public policy, financial-services reform, governance and implementation across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Iraq and Lebanon. I completed my MBA at INSEAD and hold a BS in Economics from Saint Joseph University of Beirut.

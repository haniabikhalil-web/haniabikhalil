About Hani Abi Khalil

Two sides of the same person: the work, and everything that happens around it.

Economist by training, strategy consultant by practice

My work has consistently sat at the intersection of strategy, government transformation and economic development — helping institutions translate policy and strategic ambition into operating models, governance and delivery.

I have worked across the Middle East since 2019, with governments, financial institutions and large organizations. Most of what I do starts in the same place: an institution with a clear ambition and no settled answer on how decisions get made, who owns what, and how any of it reaches the ground.

What I am known for is stakeholder management, collaborative leadership and seeing implementation through rather than stopping at the recommendation.

Background

Getting in is hard. Staying in is harder.

When I applied to consulting, it was difficult — there was no one there to guide me, and I made mistakes I probably didn't need to make.

After I joined, I ran into issues that could have been avoided with one honest conversation with the right mentor. I didn't have that person.

So I decided that no one I could help would have to deal with the same avoidable issues. I started coaching candidates alongside my consulting work, and later became an official interviewer myself.

  • Fit and behavioral interviews

Philosophy

How I approach coaching

I do not believe strong coaching is about teaching candidates to memorize answers. The objective is to understand how hiring decisions are made, identify the gaps in a candidate's profile or preparation, and build a practical strategy to close them.

The best coaching should make the candidate more independent over time — not dependent on the coach.

Coaching

Alongside consulting, I coach candidates through recruiting.

Why I started

When I applied to consulting, it was difficult — there was no one there to guide me, and I made mistakes I probably didn't need to make. After I joined, I ran into issues that could have been avoided with one honest conversation with the right mentor. I didn't have that person.

So I decided that no one I could help would have to deal with the same avoidable issues. I started coaching candidates alongside my consulting work, and later became an official interviewer myself.

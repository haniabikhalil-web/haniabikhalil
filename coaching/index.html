Getting into consulting is hard. Succeeding in it is another challenge.

I've spent years helping students, MBA candidates, and professionals prepare for consulting interviews, navigate career decisions, and grow within consulting.

Why I Coach

When I was trying to break into consulting, I made mistakes that better guidance could have helped me avoid.

Later, as a consultant and interviewer, I realized that getting the offer was only the beginning. Navigating projects, feedback, progression, and career decisions brought a different set of challenges.

I started coaching to share what I've learned, challenge people's thinking, and help them make more informed decisions.

How I Can Help

Breaking into consulting

Finding the right firms, positioning your profile, and approaching applications with a clear strategy.

Interview preparation

Preparing for case and fit interviews with practical feedback from an experienced interviewer.

Growing within consulting

Navigating staffing, performance reviews, difficult conversations, and career progression.

Career decisions & transitions

Evaluating offers, negotiating, and thinking through your next move.
